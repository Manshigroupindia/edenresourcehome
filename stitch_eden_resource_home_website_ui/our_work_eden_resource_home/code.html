school Core Program 01

Education & Academic Learning

Education is the ultimate equalizer and sovereign path toward generational transformation. We ensure every resident receives formal schooling at reputable local schools alongside dedicated evening remedial tutoring and modern computer literacy classes.

check_circle Formal School Enrollment

Tuition coverage, institutional registrations, and consistent coordination with school educators.

check_circle Study Materials & Uniforms

Complete academic supplies, winter apparel, textbooks, and daily stationery provisions.

check_circle Evening Mentorship Hours

Supervised study periods with resident educators addressing STEM and language challenges.

check_circle Computer Literacy

Fundamental digital literacy, typing practice, and guided exploratory learning sessions.

cottage Core Program 02

Nurturing Residential Care & Nutrition

Eden Resource Home is not merely a facility; it is a warm, secure family refuge in the tranquil hill station of Ukhrul. We provide comfortable, well-ventilated dormitories, balanced wholesome meals, winter woolens, and dedicated house-parents who offer continuous emotional warmth.

bed

Secure Hillside Dormitories

Segregated, well-insulated living spaces with individual bedding, personal storage lockers, and quiet study nooks.

restaurant

Nutritious Fresh Daily Meals

Locally sourced grains, seasonal vegetables, pulses, and nourishing hot soups prepared clean in our community kitchen.

family_restroom

Parental Supervision & Emotional Safety

Round-the-clock wardens and empathetic caregivers ensuring emotional stability, mutual respect, and family values.

health_and_safety Core Program 03

Healthcare, Hygiene & Child Wellbeing

Good health is the bedrock of vibrant childhood exploration. In collaboration with local medical volunteers and district health workers, we maintain active preventive health protocols, regular general wellness checkups, and routine dental screening.

medical_services

Periodic Health & Vision Check-ups

Routine developmental assessments, seasonal vaccinations, eye screenings, and basic dental hygiene consultations.

wash

Hygiene & Clean Habit Formation

Structured education on personal cleanliness, sanitary sanitation practices, hand hygiene, and disease prevention.

psychology

Emotional & Mental Wellbeing

Gentle peer circles, therapeutic counseling access, and a comforting environment free of punitive stress.

Cultural & Sports

Child Development & Extracurriculars

Every child has latent artistic talents and physical energy that deserve joyful expression. We preserve Tangkhul and Manipuri indigenous cultural traditions alongside physical sports, life skills, and speech training.

music_note Folk Music & Dance

Preserving regional heritage through traditional songs, drumming, and indigenous choreography.

sports_volleyball Football & Athletics

Daily outdoor sports on the Ukhrul fields instilling teamwork, discipline, and stamina.

mic Public Speaking

Debates, elocution, drama, and confidence-building workshops for future leadership.

handshake Life Skills Coaching
