## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dignified, deeply empathetic, and trustworthy presence for child welfare, humanitarian residential care, and education. It moves past generic non-profit tropes by pairing botanical tranquility with scholarly, editorial grace. The visual identity communicates enduring shelter, nurturing growth, institutional credibility, and warm human connection.

### Design Philosophy
- **Dignity Over Pity:** Imagery and visual tone center on joy, personal growth, educational momentum, and security, avoiding exploitative representations of distress.
- **Editorial Warmth:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke literary substance and established integrity, grounded by humanized, highly legible sans-serif body text.
- **Organic Haven:** Deep, protective botanical greens provide stable anchors, softened by warm linen-cream backdrops and gentle honey-amber accents reflecting optimism and childhood vitality.
- **Tactile Softness:** Elements feature generous radii, subtle natural borders, and gentle, luminous shadows that feel physical and safe rather than cold or hyper-digital.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from the lush mountain landscape of Ukhrul, warm sunlight, and handcrafted paper journals.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#143627`):** Deep Botanical Green. Serves as the primary anchor for dominant structural headers, primary actions, impact stat bands, and authoritative identity blocks.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F`):** Foliage Green. Applied to secondary interactive surfaces, supportive badge tags, icon backdrops, and active toggle states.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#D4A373`):** Warm Amber Sand. Used purposefully for highlight metrics, celebratory callouts, donation milestones, and key focal accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#1F2937`):** Deep Charcoal. Yields high contrast, readable typography that avoids the harshness of pitch black against warm backdrops.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7`):** Soft Warm Cream. The foundational backdrop that relieves eye strain and communicates welcoming domestic warmth.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. Reserved for elevated content cards, form wells, and active interactive plates to achieve depth.
- **Border / Divider (`#EAE5D9`):** Natural Linen. Low-contrast structural lines that organize complex metrics and modular cards without heavy visual breaks.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between the dignity of humanitarian institutional heritage and the warmth of child-focused care.

### Hierarchy & Role Allocation
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero value propositions, narrative section headings, quotes, and primary numerical metrics. The classic editorial serif conveys institutional history, care, and gravitas.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Used for narratives, card descriptions, navigation, operational metrics, and form inputs. Its open apertures and friendly curves balance readability with gentle warmth.
- **Numerical Impact Displays:** Statistics utilize `Playfair Display` bold numerals paired with uppercase `label-md` trackers in `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee instant donor readability and emotional weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid that prioritizes breathing room, visual respite, and clear content chunking.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width 1280px centered with dynamic page margins (minimum 48px). Complex sections break down into 3-column program card sets or 4-column statistic bands.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer canvas margin. Program cards and photo galleries compress into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (Below 640px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer canvas margin. Multi-column cards collapse into a single vertical sequence with unified 16px gaps.

### Spacing Principles
- Component interior padding follows strict increments: 16px (`space-md`) for compact widgets and 24px (`space-lg`) for major content and donation cards.
- Section vertical separation requires expansive rhythm (`space-xxl` to 80px) to give stories and imagery room to resonate without visual density fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, artificial drop shadows in favor of ambient, sunlit tonal layering and soft organic outlines.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FDFBF7` baseline. Used for overall canvas and wide narrative background zones.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Elevated on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px solid border in `#EAE5D9`. Ambient lift is rendered using an ultra-soft tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 54, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States):** Card elevation scales upward upon interaction: `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(20, 54, 39, 0.08)`, paired with a delicate border shift toward `#D4A373`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Panels):** High-priority donation prompts, sticky navigation bars, and lightbox components use a multi-stage ambient blur: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(20, 54, 39, 0.12)`.
- **Inverted Dark Containers:** Sections anchored by deep forest green (`#143627`) flatten surface elevation, relying on internal micro-borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and light contrast rather than shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a softened, approachable architectural curve (Level 2: 8px default, 16px large, 24px extra-large), evoking safety, embrace, and gentle craft.

### Component Radii Mapping
- **Buttons, Badges, & Small Inputs:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for disciplined structure with soft tactile touchpoints.
- **Content Cards & Image Containers:** 16px (`1rem`) to frame documentary photography with gentle warmth.
- **Hero Image Assemblies & Impact Modules:** 24px (`1.5rem`) for broad visual framing.
- **Pill Badges & Round Icon Backdrops:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly micro-indicators and status pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Button:** Solid Deep Green (`#143627`) background with white typography (`#FFFFFF`). 8px radius, height 48px, horizontal padding 24px. On hover, background shifts to Foliage Green (`#2D6A4F`) with subtle vertical translation (-1px).
- **Secondary / Donation Accent Button:** Warm Amber Sand (`#D4A373`) background with deep charcoal (`#1F2937`) text, reserved for urgent financial contributions and core support actions. Hover darkens to `#C5925E`.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#143627`, text in `#143627`. Hover fills with soft botanical wash (`rgba(20, 54, 39, 0.05)`).
- **Text Link:** Semi-bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` in `#143627` with a delicate amber underline (`#D4A373`) offset by 4px.

### Cards & Story Surfaces
- **Program & Mission Cards:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 16px radius, 1px perimeter border in `#EAE5D9`. Interior padding of 24px. Features a 48px circular icon container at the top with a pale olive tint (`rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08)`) and deep green iconography.
- **Impact Stat Cards:** Clean white or deep green background. Displays a 40px Playfair Display stat figure over a compact, tracked label in Plus Jakarta Sans. Divided by soft linen hairline dividers (`#EAE5D9`).
- **Documentary Photo Cards:** Aspect ratio 4:3 or 16:10, 16px corner radius, wrapped in an overflow-hidden frame with an optional warm gradient vignette overlay for legible caption text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** White fill, 1px border in `#EAE5D9`, 8px radius, 44px height, 16px horizontal padding. Body text in `#1F2937`. Active focus triggers a 1.5px ring in `#143627` with zero harsh shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px size, 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Border 1.5px `#143627`. Checked state fills with `#143627` and displays a crisp white check or center pip.

### Chips & Recognition Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill shape (9999px), height 28px, 12px horizontal padding. Background tinted in `#2D6A4F` at 10% opacity, label in `#143627` with 12px uppercase semibold typography.
- **Year / Milestone Badges:** Amber Sand (`#D4A373`) muted background with deep green text for anniversary marks and accreditation badges.

### Specialized NGO Components
- **Donation Tier Selector:** Horizontal button cluster with preset amounts ($25, $50, $100, Custom). Selected tier assumes `#143627` background with `#FFFFFF` text; unselected tiers remain white cards with `#EAE5D9` borders.
- **Accreditation Seal Card:** Pale parchment surface (`#F8F5EE`) bordered by a subtle double-line pattern with centered governmental recognition text and high-resolution certificates.
